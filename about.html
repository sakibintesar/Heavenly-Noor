Behind scenes 💗

Our story

"Noor" means light — and that is the whole philosophy. Every stem we choose is meant to carry a little of it into someone's day.

It started with a market run

Heavenly Noor began the way most good things do — with one bouquet made for someone we loved. Pihu Azad started wrapping market stems at her kitchen table in Boca Raton, posting the results to Instagram as @heavenly.noor.

The captions became a diary: Behind Scenes 💗 for the messy, beautiful process. Valentines 💌 for the busiest week of the year. Details 🌷 for the tiny things — ribbon texture, stem angle, the way light hits a petal.

How we work

Petals, step by step

1 · You reach out

WhatsApp, Instagram DM, or the contact form. Tell us the occasion, palette and budget.

2 · We source

Morning market runs — whatever was most beautiful that day, plus the premium stems your design calls for.

3 · We design

Every bouquet is built by hand in the studio — no foam bricks, no shortcuts. Just mechanics, stems and light.

4 · We deliver

"Flowers that feel gathered, not manufactured."

— The Heavenly Noor promise
